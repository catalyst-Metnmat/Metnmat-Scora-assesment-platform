Guidelines

Before you start

  • Fill in your employee profile first — HR uses it to match the assessment to the right role.
  • Rate yourself from 0 to 5 on every skill. Enter 0 explicitly where there is no exposure — do not skip any skill.
  • Use the evidence field to note projects, tools, certifications, or examples that prove the rating.
  • Rate honestly. Every rating is validated in a follow-up interview, and over-claiming counts against the review.
  • Your progress is saved automatically on this device — close the page and continue any time.
  • One submission per employee per assessment cycle. Contact HR if your assessment needs to be redone.
